About LLA

Beyond Academics.

Living Learning Academy (LLA) is a values-driven learning network offering expert-led curriculum, trusted mentors and hands-on experiences that equip young people ages 6–18 with life skills, leadership, independent thinking and bold character.

Our Vision

By partnering with families, educators and alternative schools, we prepare the next generation to thrive in every sector of society while upholding traditional values that helped build Canada.

The word education comes from roots meaning to raise, nourish and lead forth. Educating the next generation has never been values-neutral. LLA goes beyond academics openly. Together, we are leading forth a generation ready to build a stronger Canada.

Values You Can Trust

Every program clearly identifies its foundation before your family chooses it.

  • CW: Christian worldview. Explicitly incorporates a Christian worldview. You don’t have to be Christian to take part: everyone is welcome to learn with us.
  • CV: Core values. Not explicitly faith-based, but grounded in C.L.I.M.B., LLA’s Core Values and Code.

Some programs are offered in both versions (CW & CV), so your family can choose.

The LLA Approach

A New Way to Educate… That’s Actually Old.

The word education comes from roots meaning to raise, nourish and lead forth. Every educational system leads children somewhere, even those that describe themselves as secular. At LLA, we’re upfront about the values guiding ours.

We believe raising a well-rounded next generation takes more than academics. It takes character, practical skills, discernment and a sense of duty to God, family, community and country. And it takes a village of parents and skilled mentors working together to pass down the knowledge, wisdom and values that prepare young people for life.

We’re bringing that village back.

Through our values-driven learning network, specializing in life skills, leadership and character development through hands-on, real-world experiences.

Leave the academics to the schools.

We’ll take care of the rest.
