The LLA Code

Our Code for Learners & Families

A shared standard for how we learn, treat one another and grow together.

Every learner and family who joins LLA commits to this Code. It is built on C.L.I.M.B., the framework behind every LLA course and program. Mentors follow a Code of their own.

For Learners: I will

  • C
    CharacterBe honest, kind and respectful to my mentors, classmates and the places we learn in.
  • L
    LeadershipTake responsibility for my choices, help others and lead by example.
  • I
    Independent thinkingAsk good questions, listen to different views and think for myself.
  • M
    Mastery of life skillsCome ready, try my best and keep practicing until the skill is mine.
  • B
    Building impactServe others and leave every group and place better than I found it.

Safe & Respectful Learning

  • I follow safety instructions and use tools and equipment only as my mentor directs.
  • I treat everyone with dignity. Bullying, harassment and put-downs have no place at LLA.
  • I keep phones and devices away unless a mentor says they are part of the class.
  • I tell a mentor or LLA right away if something does not feel safe or right.

For Families: We will

  • Remain our child’s primary educator. LLA courses and programs add to our family’s role in our child’s education and care. They do not replace it.
  • Share what matters. We give LLA accurate registration, health and emergency contact information, and update it when it changes.
  • Be reliable. We arrive and pick up on time, and let LLA know ahead when a learner will be away.
  • Communicate with respect. We bring questions and concerns to the mentor or to LLA directly and kindly.
  • Choose with care. We read each course’s description and its CW or CV label, and we respect every family’s beliefs in the group.
  • Back the Code at home. We talk with our learner about this Code and encourage them to live it.

When the Code Is Not Followed

LLA will first talk with the learner and family. Where safety or the learning of others is affected, LLA may pause or end a learner’s participation, in line with our Registration Terms.
