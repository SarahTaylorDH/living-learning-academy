Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Draft: this policy is under review and may be updated.

Living Learning Academy (“LLA”, “we”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through this website, why, and the choices you have. We follow Canada’s federal privacy law, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • On every form: your name, email, postal code, city and province or territory, and, if you choose, a cell phone number.
  • Parents: each child’s age group, gender and interests (never names), how your children are schooled, the formats and weekdays that suit you, and anything you write in the open questions.
  • Mentors: the areas you could teach and why you’d like to help.
  • Community, volunteer and course requests: which private community you (or your teen, aged 13 or older, with your permission) would like to join, topics or events you’d like, how you’d like to help, and the courses and number of students you have in mind.
  • Your choices: whether you want event notices, email updates or text messages, and when you agreed to this policy.

We ask you not to include children’s names or health information in open answers. Answers about your family’s values are optional.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to you, and to confirm by email that we received your form.
  • To contact you about the courses, programs, mentoring, volunteering and events you asked about.
  • To plan which courses and events to offer, and where. Your postal code helps us see where interest is clustered.
  • To invite you, and your teen if you gave permission, to our private online communities when they open, and to review those requests.

We only send news by email, or text messages, if you ticked the box asking for them, and you can opt out at any time.

How we protect it

Your information is stored securely with our database provider, Supabase, and confirmation emails are sent through an email service provider. Our private communities are hosted on Facebook, which has its own privacy policy. Only authorized LLA staff can view it. The website itself can only add new sign-ups and cannot read existing ones. We do not sell or rent your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, at any time by emailing info@livinglearningacademy.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page.
