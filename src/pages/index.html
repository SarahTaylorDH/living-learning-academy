Our Vision

Beyond Academics.

Living Learning Academy (LLA) is a values-driven learning network for youth ages 6–18. Through expert-led curriculum, trusted mentors and hands-on experiences, we equip you with life skills, leadership, independent thinking and bold character.

By partnering with families, educators and alternative schools, we prepare the next generation to thrive in every sector of society while upholding traditional values that helped build Canada.

The LLA Approach

A New Way to Educate… That’s Actually Old.

The word education comes from roots meaning to raise, nourish and lead forth. Every educational system leads children somewhere, even those that describe themselves as secular. At LLA, we’re upfront about the values guiding ours.

We believe raising a well-rounded next generation takes more than academics. It takes character, practical skills, discernment and a sense of duty to God, family, community and country. And it takes a village of parents and skilled mentors working together to pass down the knowledge, wisdom and values that prepare you for life, from age 6 to 18.

We’re bringing that village back.

Through our values-driven learning network, specializing in life skills, leadership and character development through hands-on, real-world experiences.

Leave the academics to the schools.

We’ll take care of the rest.

A Bible for Every Student.

At LLA, we believe there’s no better guide than God’s Word for wisdom, good character, stewardship and leading well in life. Our goal is simple: every student who wants a Bible should have one.

Here are two ways you can help:

Share a Bible

Send us a like-new NIV or NLT Bible made for children or youth, such as a kids’ or student edition, with no writing inside. In some areas, we can arrange a pickup.
