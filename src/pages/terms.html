Using this website

This website shares information about Living Learning Academy (“LLA”) and lets you sign up to hear about courses, mentoring, community and events. Please use it lawfully and provide accurate information when you sign up.

Courses and events

Joining the waitlist does not reserve a place or create a booking. Course themes, dates, locations and prices may change before registration opens. Paid course registrations follow the Registration Terms below.

Registration Terms

These terms apply when you register a child for an LLA course.

Payment

You can pay in full by Interac e-Transfer or card, or by card in monthly payments, which end automatically after the last payment. Card payments are processed securely by Square. When you choose e-Transfer, your child’s spot is held for 12 hours while we wait for payment. Prices include everything; there is no tax added.

Family discount

Each additional course registered by the same household in the same term is 10% off. The discount resets each term.

If a course doesn’t run

Each course needs 10 students to run. If a course doesn’t reach 10 students, or LLA has to cancel it, you’ll get a full refund or can transfer to another course.

Cancelling

Course registrations are non-refundable. If you cancel at least 30 days before the first class, you’ll receive a credit for the full amount you paid toward a future LLA course. Cancellations within 30 days of the first class aren’t normally eligible for credit, but in cases of illness or a family emergency, please reach out: we’ll gladly consider a credit. Missed classes can’t be refunded or credited.

If you’re on a monthly plan, cancelling doesn’t stop the remaining payment; it counts toward your credit when one applies.

To cancel, email info@livinglearningacademy.com.
