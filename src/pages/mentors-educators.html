Course categories

  • Critical Thinking & Debate

  • Finance & Entrepreneurship

    Manage money wisely and turn ideas into opportunities.

  • Public Speaking & Influence

    Speak clearly, communicate courageously and influence with integrity.

  • Impact & Community

    Put compassion and leadership into action through service.

  • Faith & Discipleship

    Understand and defend the Christian faith, and grow as a follower of Christ in God’s will.

  • Culture & Identity

  • Civics, Freedom & Rights

Hands-on programs

  • Survival, Nature & Character

    Build outdoor skills, resilience and resourcefulness in nature.

  • Farming, Food & Character

    Learn to prepare and grow food, care for animals and steward the land.

  • Build, Serve & Character

    Designed for boys: use tools, build and make repairs, and grow in godly masculinity through manners, respect and service.

  • Self-Defence & Character

    Develop personal safety skills, sound judgment and self-control.

  • Music Theatre & Character

    Sing, act and perform on stage, building confidence, discipline and teamwork.

  • Dance & Character

    Build discipline, teamwork and confidence through dance and movement.

  • Film, Television & Character

    Create shows and documentaries, from filming to editing, and tell stories with integrity.

  • Journalism, Media & Character

    Report the truth through news writing, podcasts and media, with courage and integrity.

  • Beauty & Character

    Explore hair, makeup and personal care grounded in dignity, self-respect and biblical beauty.

  • Etiquette, Charm & Character

    Designed for girls: grow in godly femininity through grace, good manners and social confidence.

  • Firearm Safety & Character

    Learn safe handling, responsibility and an understanding of Canada’s firearm laws.
