The LLA Mentor Code

The standard every LLA Mentor agrees to before leading learners.

LLA Mentors are the principled instructors who lead our courses and programs. Families trust them with their children, so every Mentor agrees to this Code. It is built on C.L.I.M.B., the framework behind everything we teach. Learners and families follow the LLA Code for Learners & Families.

As an LLA Mentor, I will

  1. Put safety first. I protect every learner’s physical and emotional safety. I follow LLA’s safety rules and emergency procedures, complete the screening and training LLA requires, keep learners within sight of other adults, and report any injury or concern to LLA right away.
  2. Teach with integrity. I teach each course as it is described and as it is labelled, CW or CV. I am honest about my experience and credentials, and I represent LLA truthfully.
  3. Respect every learner. I treat each learner with dignity and patience. I do not shame, ridicule, harass or play favourites, and I never use my position to pressure a learner.
  4. Keep healthy boundaries. I communicate with learners in the open, through parents or LLA’s channels, never in private. I stay free of alcohol, drugs and vaping while working with learners.
  5. Partner with families. I respect parents as their children’s primary educators. I share progress and concerns promptly, kindly and honestly.
  6. Invite thinking. I present ideas fairly, welcome questions, and let learners think for themselves, including on matters of belief.
  7. Protect privacy. I keep learner and family information confidential. I take photos or video only as LLA and families have approved.
  8. Model C.L.I.M.B. I show the character, leadership, independent thinking, life skills and service I ask of learners, and I keep growing as a mentor.

Accountability

A Mentor who does not follow this Code may be removed from LLA programs. LLA may act immediately where a learner’s safety is at risk.
