    Good to Know

    Before you register

    • What's included7 two-hour classes on Fridays from January 8 to February 19, 2027, plus an optional field trip on February 26.
    • Minimum class sizeEach course needs 10 students to run. If it doesn't fill, you'll get a full refund or can transfer to another course.
    • MaterialsCharged separately, only if a course needs them, and listed at registration.
    • Field tripThe last class is an optional field trip. Extra fees may apply, with fundraising options available.
    • Support for every learnerTrained support workers join larger classes and classes with children who have additional needs. Families may bring their own, after a criminal record check.
    • PaymentAll registration details are needed before payment. Interac e-Transfer is preferred; debit and credit cards are accepted through Square. e-Transfer spots are held for 12 hours; card payments secure your spot right away. We'll email a thank-you with the campus address and a short form for your child's details, emergency contacts and permissions.
