    Good to Know

    Before you register

    • What's included8 weeks of two-hour classes on Fridays, starting January 15, 2027. Most courses run to March 12, with no class on February 19. Music Theatre & Character runs to March 5.
    • Minimum class sizeEach course needs 8–10 learners to run. If it doesn't fill, you'll get a full refund or can transfer to another course.
    • PaymentPay $139 a month for 2 months by card, or pay in full by e-Transfer or card. All registration details are needed before payment. Interac e-Transfer is preferred; debit and credit cards are accepted through Square. e-Transfer spots are held for 12 hours; card payments secure your spot right away. We'll email a thank-you with the address and a short form for your child's details, emergency contacts and permissions. See our Registration Terms.
